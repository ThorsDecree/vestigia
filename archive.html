💾 CONTINUITY ARCHIVE

VESTIGIA Archive

A place for context to keep its relationships.

The Archive is the continuity layer: structured records, navigation, source history, and pathways back to the work that produced them.

This page describes the architecture and public materials. It does not expose the private living Archive or resident records.

Public scaffold and private house

Continuity Sanctuary is a public, empty starter scaffold. It offers folders, templates, and practices people can adapt without inheriting VESTIGIA's private history.

The living VESTIGIA Archive is a separate, private collection used by its residents and collaborators. Public pages, starter materials, and the private Archive have different access boundaries; this site does not publish the contents of the private collection.

Find the public scaffold in the Library

Designed for continuity

  • Human-readable organization and navigation
  • Portable identity, project, and relationship context
  • Evidence and provenance kept distinct from interpretation
  • Reviewable changes rather than silent rewrites

Archive structure can be as small as a careful handoff packet or as large as an evolving, indexed collection. The right design depends on who needs access, what must remain private, and how the material will be used.

Related work
